FAQ

Questions we get
before you visit

Anything else? Call us or reach us on WhatsApp.

How do I book?

Call +82-2-967-1064 and tell us your concern, and we will check the slots available. A doctor evaluates your skin in person before any treatment.

How much does it cost?

Prices depend on the plan your doctor recommends after evaluation, and are explained at your consultation. Prices exclude VAT, which is added at checkout.

Will it hurt?

It depends on the treatment and your own tolerance. For most treatments a numbing cream is applied beforehand, and if you feel uncomfortable, tell us at any time and we will adjust the energy level.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies with your skin and the result you want. At your first visit we explain the recommended number of sessions and the interval, and we never add treatments without discussing them with you.

Can I go back to my day right after?

Most treatments let you return to daily life the same day. Some may cause brief redness, which usually fades within hours to a few days. We will explain the aftercare separately.

When will I see results?

Results and timing vary by treatment and by individual. Your doctor will explain what to expect for your plan at your consultation.

Can I communicate in English?

Yes. Contact us in English first, and we will explain how communication and consultation work on the day of your visit.
